# Data safety form answers for PureBurn

These match how the app works today. If you add a feature that sends data anywhere (login, cloud backup, ads, analytics, crash reports, a server), update this file and the privacy policy (site/privacy.html) first, then answer the Play form again before releasing.

| Question in Play Console | Answer |
|---|---|
| Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types? | No |
| Is all of the user data collected by your app encrypted in transit? | Not applicable, no data leaves the device |
| Do you provide a way for users to request that their data is deleted? | Not applicable, nothing is collected. The privacy policy explains how to clear data on the device |
| Does the app use third-party SDKs that collect data? | No |

Why the answer is "No data collected": PureBurn stores the profile, goal, exercise ticks, meals, water, steps and weight entries only in the app's local storage (shared_preferences). Nothing is transmitted off the device.

Data that stays on the device (for your reference, not declared as collected): sex, age, height, weight, activity level, goal, target weight, workout days, exercise place, food budget, daily ticks, water, steps, weight history.
