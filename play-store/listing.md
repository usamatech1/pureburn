## Full description (max 4000 characters)
PureBurn is a clean, black and white health companion. Enter your details once and get a plan made for you: your BMI, a healthy weight range, a weekly exercise plan, and a Pakistani diet plan with real food and real prices.

WHAT YOU GET
- BMI check with a South Asian standard (health risks begin at a lower BMI) or the WHO standard
- Your best weight and healthy range for your height
- A goal: lose, maintain or gain weight, with a target weight and a pace you choose
- A weekly exercise plan for 3, 4 or 5 days, at home or at a gym
- A simple diet plan with everyday Pakistani food such as roti, daal, sabzi, dahi and eggs, with an estimated cost per day and per month
- Daily tracking: tick off your exercises and meals, log water and steps
- Weight log with a progress chart
- Light and dark mode

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account. No ads. No tracking. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

IMPORTANT
PureBurn gives general guidance only and is not a medical device. BMI is not accurate for athletes or pregnant women. Please talk to a doctor before starting a new exercise or diet plan.
