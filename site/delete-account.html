Delete your PureBurn account

PureBurn app for Android and the PureBurn website

You can delete your PureBurn account and the data stored in it at any time. You only have an account if you chose to sign in with an email address or a Google account. If you never signed in, PureBurn has no account or cloud data for you.

Option 1: delete it in the app (fastest)

  1. Open PureBurn and go to the Account page.
  2. Press Delete my account and confirm.

If the app says it needs a fresh sign-in, press Sign out, sign in again, then press Delete my account again.

Option 2: ask us to delete it

If you cannot open the app, for example because you uninstalled it or lost your phone, send an email to thesyedu@gmail.com with the subject "PureBurn account deletion". Write from the email address you used to sign up, or tell us which email address your account uses, so we can check that it is yours. We will delete your account and cloud data and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your account (your email address and sign-in).
  • Your cloud backup: profile, daily logs, weights, measurements, food and workout logs, custom foods and settings.
  • Your challenge scores and display name.

Deleting is permanent and cannot be undone. Questions you sent to the AI coach are not stored by PureBurn, so there is nothing of them to delete on our side.

What is not deleted

Data saved on your own phone (or in your browser) is not removed when you delete your account, because it is yours and never left your device. To remove it, clear the app's data in your phone settings or uninstall the app.
