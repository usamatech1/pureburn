PureBurn Privacy Policy

Effective date: 31 October 2026

PureBurn is a free BMI, goal, daily exercise and Pakistani diet tracker. This page explains what happens to your information when you use the PureBurn app for Android or the PureBurn website.

The short version

  • You can use PureBurn without an account. Press "Skip for now" and your information stays on your own device.
  • If you choose to sign in, your data can be backed up to your account and synced between your phones. The AI coach and challenges also need an account.
  • We do not show ads, we do not use advertising or analytics tools, and we do not sell your information.
  • Some of your information is health related (weight, height, sleep, and optionally blood sugar and blood pressure readings). We treat it as sensitive and only use it to run the features you use.

Information you enter, and where it is stored

To work out your BMI, calorie target, exercise plan and diet plan, the app asks for details such as your sex, age, height, weight, daily activity level, goal, target weight, workout days, where you exercise, your food budget, diet type and food allergies. While you use it, you can also add exercises, meals, water, steps, sleep times and quality, weight entries, body measurements, workout sets, custom foods, and (if you turn on health tracking) blood sugar and blood pressure readings.

By default all of this is saved only on your device, in the app's own storage (on the website, in your browser's local storage). It is not sent to us. If you use an app lock (PIN or fingerprint), the check happens on your device.

If you create an account (optional)

You can sign in with an email address and password, or with your Google account. We use Google Firebase for this. What is collected and stored:

  • Account details: your email address, and a unique account ID. If you use Google sign-in, Google shares your email address with the app. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see or store your password.
  • Cloud backup and sync: when you are signed in and sync is on, a full copy of your PureBurn data (everything described above, including your profile, daily logs, weights, measurements, food and workout logs, custom foods, and your settings such as diet type, allergies and sleep goal) is saved to Google Cloud Firestore under your account. It is protected by rules so that only your own signed-in account can read or write it. Your app lock PIN is not part of this backup. You can switch automatic sync off on the Account page.

Challenges

If you join a challenge, your chosen display name (up to 20 characters) and your score are stored and are visible to other signed-in PureBurn users in that challenge. Use a name that does not identify you if you prefer. Leaving a challenge removes your entry.

AI coach (optional, needs an account)

When you ask the coach a question, the app sends your question (up to 600 characters) together with a short summary of your data to our coach service. The summary can include your goal, sex, age, height, weight, BMI, calorie target, today's meals, water, steps, diet type, allergies, Ramadan mode and activity level. If you have turned on health tracking, it can also include your latest blood sugar and blood pressure readings.

The coach service runs on Cloudflare Workers. It checks that you are signed in, passes your question and the summary to Google's Gemini API to write the answer, and returns the answer to you. The service keeps only a daily question counter linked to your account, used to apply daily limits. It does not keep your questions or the answers. Google processes the request under its own Gemini API terms (see ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms), which for free-tier use may allow Google to review requests to improve its services. Because of this, please do not type your name, phone number or other private details into the coach. If you are not signed in, the app answers simple questions from your plan on the device and nothing is sent.

Device permissions (all optional)

  • Health Connect (steps and sleep): if you allow it, PureBurn reads your steps and sleep sessions from Health Connect on your device. This is read-only. The values are saved into your PureBurn log, so they are included in your cloud backup if you sync.
  • Physical activity: used to count steps with your phone's step sensor. The count stays in your PureBurn log.
  • Notifications: used for reminders you set up (for example water, meals, workouts and bedtime). Reminders are created on your device.
  • Biometric or PIN lock: checked on your device only.

You can withdraw any permission at any time in your phone's settings.

These companies may process information in countries other than yours, including the United States. We do not sell your information and we do not share it with anyone else, except if the law requires it. Data is sent between the app and these services over HTTPS.

The website

The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any website host, GitHub may keep standard server logs such as your IP address when you open the page, under GitHub's own privacy statement. The web version may also load fonts or rendering files from Google-hosted servers. Without an account, the details you type in are kept only in your browser.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Data on your device: on Android, open Settings, then Apps, then PureBurn, then Storage, and choose Clear data, or uninstall the app. On the website, clear the site data for this page in your browser.
  • Data in your account: clearing the data on your device does not remove the copy in your account. To have your account and all the data stored in it deleted, including your challenge entries, send us a deletion request using the contact below. We will delete your cloud data and account. Once deleted, the information cannot be recovered.

We keep your cloud data for as long as your account exists.

Children

PureBurn is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Health information notice

PureBurn gives general guidance only. BMI is not accurate for athletes, pregnant women and some other groups, and the calorie, exercise and diet suggestions, including the AI coach's answers, are estimates. PureBurn is not a medical device and does not give medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Please talk to a doctor or qualified professional before starting a new exercise or diet plan, especially if you have a medical condition.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above. If PureBurn starts collecting new kinds of information, this page will be updated before that change is released.

Contact

For questions about this policy, or to ask for your account and data to be deleted, please open an issue at github.com/usamatech1/pureburn/issues. Issues are public, so do not post private details there; write only that you want to make a privacy request and we will tell you how to continue.
