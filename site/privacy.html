PureBurn Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 October 2026

PureBurn is a free BMI, goal, daily exercise and Pakistani diet tracker. This page explains what happens to your information when you use the PureBurn app for Android or the PureBurn website.

The short version

PureBurn does not have accounts, does not collect your personal information on any server, does not show ads and does not use analytics or tracking. Everything you enter stays on your own device.

What you enter, and where it is stored

To work out your BMI, calorie target, exercise plan and diet plan, the app asks for details such as your sex, age, height, weight, daily activity level, goal, target weight, workout days, where you exercise, your food budget, and the exercises, meals, water and steps you tick off each day, plus your weight entries.

This information is saved only on your device, in the app's own storage (on the website, in your browser's local storage). It is never sent to us or to any other company. We cannot see it.

What we do not do

  • We do not create accounts or ask you to sign in.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or files.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics, crash reporting or other third-party tracking tools in the app.
  • We do not sell or share your information.

The website

The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any website host, GitHub may keep standard server logs such as your IP address when you open the page, under GitHub's own privacy statement. The web version may also load fonts or rendering files from Google-hosted servers. The details you type in are still kept only in your browser.

Deleting your data

Because the data is only on your device, you are in full control. On Android, open Settings, then Apps, then PureBurn, then Storage, and choose Clear data, or uninstall the app. On the website, clear the site data for this page in your browser. Once cleared, the information cannot be recovered.

Children

PureBurn is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Health information notice

PureBurn gives general guidance only. BMI is not accurate for athletes, pregnant women and some other groups, and the calorie, exercise and diet suggestions are estimates. PureBurn is not a medical device and does not give medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Please talk to a doctor or qualified professional before starting a new exercise or diet plan, especially if you have a medical condition.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above. If the app ever starts collecting information, this page will be updated before that change is released.

Contact

For questions about this policy, please open an issue at github.com/usamatech1/pureburn/issues.
